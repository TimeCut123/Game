# Echoes

**Your past is the enemy.**

Every orb you grab summons an *echo* — a ghost of you that retraces your exact path from the very start of the run. Echoes pile up, each one further behind you in time. Touch any of them and you're caught.

## Play
Open `index.html` in any browser. No install, no build.

- **Move:** WASD / arrow keys, or drag anywhere (touch joystick)
- **Pause:** P / Esc · **Mute:** M

## Rules
- Gold orb: +10 points, and spawns a new echo at your starting point.
- Near miss with an echo: +2 points.
- Purple shard (every 5 orbs): shatters all echoes near you.
- Dotted lines show where each echo is heading next — it's just your own old path.
- Standing still is deadly: your echoes will arrive where you are.

Strategy: plan your route, because every move you make now becomes an obstacle later.
